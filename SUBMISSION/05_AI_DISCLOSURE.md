# AI usage disclosure

AI was used extensively and transparently for: analyzing the VDR requirements and architecture, creating risk-based test ideas and permission matrices, scaffolding and debugging Jest/Supertest, Playwright and Cypress tests, building safe environment/setup scripts and evidence aggregation, performing source-assisted investigations, and drafting findings/test plan/QA report.

Human execution and oversight: the operator installed tools and ran selected suites on Ubuntu, supplied real console output, selected priorities, and challenged AI-generated claims. Prior AI-authored bug lists were **not treated as proof**; issues are classified as reproduced with a stated boundary, source-review concern, or unverified hypothesis. Original product unit tests remain credited to the upstream developers. All generated tests and recommendations merit human review.
